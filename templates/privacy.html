Privacy & terms

Last updated: {{ updated|date:"j F Y" }}

What Sreeva stores

  • Your account: name, username, email and your password (stored only as a secure one-way hash — nobody can read it).
  • Your studio: studio name, phone, address, team and equipment.
  • What you enter for your work: customer names and phone numbers, event dates and places, prices and payments.
  • Security log: logins and important changes with time and IP address, to protect your account.

How it is used

  • Only to run your studio’s bookings, payments and reminders.
  • Never sold or shared. No advertising and no tracking — cookies are used only to keep you logged in securely.
  • Emails are sent only to confirm your email and to help you recover your login.

Who can see it

  • Only people in your own studio: the owner sees everything; staff see their shoots but no money.
  • Other studios can never see your data.
  • The site maintainer can access the server only to keep it running and to restore backups.

Where it is kept

On PythonAnywhere’s servers, sent over HTTPS. A copy of the database is saved daily (the last 30 are kept). Owners can download all their data as an Excel file any time from Studio settings → Backup & export.

Your customers’ details

Studios add their customers’ names and phone numbers. Please add only what you need for the booking and tell customers you keep them for their booking and reminders.

Deleting your data

Owners can delete bookings and payments themselves.{% if contact %} To delete your whole account and studio, email {{ contact }} from your account’s email address.{% endif %}

Terms of use

  • Sreeva is free and provided “as is”. We work to keep it running and your data safe, but cannot guarantee it will never be unavailable — keep your own Excel backup now and then.
  • Use it only for genuine studio work. Accounts used for spam or abuse may be closed.
  • The “Try the demo” studio is shared sample data and is reset every day — don’t enter real details there.
  • If these terms change, the new version will appear on this page.
